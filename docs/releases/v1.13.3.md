# arg-bridge v1.13.3

This release stabilizes the tmux client-disconnect regression test. Product
behavior is unchanged from 1.13.2.

## Fixed

- The disconnect fixture now keeps its synthetic idle watchdog alive beyond
  the first silent-paste recovery window. A heavily loaded runner can lose the
  initial paste after a client disconnect; the previous 1.5-second idle budget
  ended the fixture before the 3-second recovery attempt ran.

## Compatibility

- `/goal resume` continuation steering, third-party Responses provider
  steering, and all 1.13.2 behavior are unchanged.

## Validation

- 1,190 ArgBridge tests passed; 11 environment-dependent tests skipped.
- 27 vendored MixAgents Broker tests passed.
- The disconnect test passed after exercising the full silent-paste recovery
  window instead of stopping at the previous 1.5-second idle boundary.
